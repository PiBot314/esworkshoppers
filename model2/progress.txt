1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & SYSTEM OVERVIEW
====================================================================================================
The Net-Zero Edge AI Building Management project forecasts and balances three electrical domains:
  1. Solar Yield Generation (SL Sensors): Rooftop and campus photovoltaic installations.
  2. Main Substation & Grid Draw (EM Sensors): Incomer electricity and transformer loads.
  3. Room & Server Demand (SR-EM Sensors): High-density computer lab and server room consumption.

Target Deployment Platform:
  Qualcomm Innovators Development Kit (QIDK) equipped with Qualcomm Snapdragon SoC and
  Hexagon NPU / DSP. Edge inference must execute locally within strict memory, latency, and
  static-tensor constraints.

All telemetry has been systematically cleaned, calibrated, unit-standardized to Watts (W),
resampled to uniform 15-minute intervals, augmented with Indian Standard Time (IST) cyclic
features, normalized to [0, 1], benchmarked against statistical baselines, trained via PyTorch
LSTMs (single-node and unified 58-feature all-sensors), and packaged into static-shape ONNX models
with on-device ADB runners.

2. SENSOR NETWORK & PARAMETER DICTIONARY
====================================================================================================
The campus IoT sensor infrastructure comprises three primary device families:

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. SL SENSORS (Solar Plant Inverters & Multi-Function Energy Meters)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Monitors solar PV production across buildings (Nilgiri, Vindhya, Palash).

  - VoltageR_N, VoltageY_N, VoltageB_N : AC Phase-to-Neutral voltages for Red, Yellow, Blue (V).
  - Voltage_avg                        : Average of the three phase-to-neutral voltages (V).
  - VoltageR_Y, VoltageY_B, VoltageB_R : Line-to-Line AC voltages (~400V - 415V).
  - Voltage_RS, Voltage_ST, Voltage_TR : Line-to-Line notation used in inverter firmware (V).
  - Voltage1, Voltage2, Voltage3       : AC output voltage per inverter phase (V).
  - Current1, Current2, Current3       : AC output current per inverter phase (A).
  - Power1, Power2, Power3             : AC active power per inverter phase (W or kW).
  - RPhaseLC, YPhaseLC, BPhaseLC       : Line current flowing through phase conductors (A).
  - NPhaseLC / neutral_current_A       : Neutral line return current; measures phase unbalance (A).
  - RPhaseactC, YPhaseactC, BPhaseactC : Active (in-phase) current component (I * cos φ) (A).
  - RPhaseRactC, YPhaseRactC, ...      : Reactive (quadrature) current component (I * sin φ) (A).
  - Q1PF, Q2PF, Q3PF                   : 4-Quadrant displacement power factor per phase.
  - PF_Avg / Power Factor              : Overall average displacement power factor.
  - R/Y/B_Active_power                 : Real power per phase (kW).
  - 3_Active_power / ActivePower       : Total 3-phase real solar power generated (Target, converted to W).
  - 3_Reactive_power                   : Total 3-phase reactive power (kVAr, converted to VAr).
  - 3_Apparent_power                   : Total 3-phase apparent power (kVA).
  - Frequency                          : Grid electrical frequency (nominal 50.0 Hz).
  - TEI                                : Total Energy Imported (kWh); night-time auxiliary consumption.
  - TEE                                : Total Energy Exported (kWh); lifetime cumulative generation.
  - Eac_today                          : Daily cumulative energy generation since midnight reset (kWh).
  - Eac_total                          : Inverter lifetime total energy generated (kWh).
  - PV1Voltage - PV6Voltage            : DC voltage produced by solar PV array strings 1 to 6 (V).
  - PV1Current - PV6Current            : DC current generated by solar PV array strings 1 to 6 (A).
  - PV1Power - PV6Power                : DC electrical power from strings 1 to 6 (W).

  - *Power Factor                      : Power factor ratio; indicates inductive motor loads.
  - *Total Energy                      : Cumulative active energy counter (Wh or kWh).
  - R/Y/B Current (on KH95)            : Individual phase currents (A).
  - R/Y/B Power (on KH95)              : Individual phase active powers (W).
  - power_total_W                      : Sum of R, Y, and B active phase powers (Target, W).

3. INITIAL DATA AUDIT: FLAWS IDENTIFIED IN RAW / EARLY DATA
====================================================================================================
When auditing by_version/ and the early lstm_ready/ directories, several critical errors were uncovered:

1. High Volume of Unresolved NaNs in Time Series:
   - SL-NI01-00 had 32.54% NaNs (10,757 missing steps).
   - SR-EM-KH04-00, 01, 02 had 17.13% NaNs (6,004 missing steps each).
   - EM-CR-KH95-00 had 16.55% NaNs (5,633 missing steps).
   - EM-CR-SB00-00 had 10.32% NaNs (3,395 missing steps).
   *Cause:* The raw CSVs in by_version only logged events when sensors were transmitting. Whenever
   a sensor lost power or connectivity for days, no rows existed. Resampling to a continuous 15-minute
   grid exposed these outages as NaNs. The preliminary ffill(limit=4) only filled up to 1 hour, leaving
   multi-day network outages as NaNs. Feeding NaNs into an LSTM causes gradients and cell states to explode (loss = nan).

2. Inverted Power Sign on Solar Plant SL-PL00-00:
   - In SL-PL00-00, active power was 97.5% negative (ranging from -65.8 kW to 0.0 kW).
   *Cause:* The physical Current Transformers (CTs) were oriented toward the building bus, registering
   solar export as reverse current.

3. Unit Inconsistency Across Nodes (Watts vs. Kilowatts):
   - SL-PL00-00, SL-VN95-00, SL-NI01-00 reported in Kilowatts (active_power_kW, 0 - 150 kW).
   - SL-NI03-00, SL-NI03-01, SL-VN02-01 reported in Watts (active_power_W, 0 - 70,000 W).
   - Comparing or transferring models across nodes had a 1000x scaling mismatch.

4. Firmware Register Transposition in SR-EM-KH04-01 & SR-EM-KH04-02:
   - In by_version, the column labeled "Current" contained values of ~235 - 250 (which is Mains Voltage!).
   - The column labeled "Frequency" contained values of ~0.02 - 0.25 (which is Circuit Current in Amps!).
   - The column labeled "Power Factor" contained values of ~49.9 - 50.1 (which is Grid Frequency in Hz!).
   - In early lstm_ready, "Current" was renamed to voltage_V, but "Frequency" (~0.02 A) was mislabeled
     as freq_col_raw, while real grid frequency was completely discarded.

5. Unnormalized Data Crashing Neural Gate Activations:
   - Features spanned vastly different numerical ranges: gd_power_W (~886,000 W), voltage_V (~240 V),
     current_A (~0.02 A), and cyclic features ([-1, 1]).
   - Unscaled values of 800,000 immediately saturate LSTM sigmoid gates (σ(z) = 1.0, σ'(z) = 0),
     completely halting weight updates due to vanishing gradients.

4. STEP-BY-STEP TRANSFORMATIONS: WHAT, WHY, AND HOW
====================================================================================================

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ISSUE 1: Resolving NaNs - Calculating P = I * V vs. Filling 0
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Question: Can missing power be calculated from Voltage and Current (P = I * V)?
  * Audit Finding: We analyzed every timestamp where Power was NaN. In 99.9% of instances, Voltage and
    Current were ALSO NaN simultaneously because the gateway/meter was unpowered and completely offline.
    Because V and I did not exist, P could not be computed.
  * Solution Applied: In strict compliance with course TA guidelines, all remaining missing timestamps
    during extended outages were filled with 0.0. Target coverage across all 11 nodes is now 100.0%.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ISSUE 2: Negative Solar Generation - Calculating via Other Parameters vs. Absolute Value
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Question: Can negative power in SL-PL00-00 be calculated from other parameters?
  * Audit Finding: We examined the raw electrical registers in V3.0.0.parquet. Line currents were
    positive (0 - 97.4 A) and voltages were positive (~230 V). Active currents (RPhaseactC, etc.)
    were negative (-18 A avg, min -97.5 A) because the current vector was 180° out of phase with voltage.
    Calculating power via P = |3 * V_avg * I_active| produces the exact same numerical magnitude
    as |3_Active_power| (~65.8 kW peak).
  * Solution Applied: Applied .abs() across physical power channels. This rectified the inverted export
    polarity on SL-PL00-00 and cleared small nighttime sensor calibration noise (-0.2 kW / -5 W) on other nodes.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ISSUE 3: Unit Standardization to Watts
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * What: Multiplied all kW and kVAr fields by 1000.0.
  * Renamed:
      active_power_kW     -> active_power_W
      reactive_power_kVAr -> reactive_power_VAr
  * Result: All 11 nodes now uniformly express target power in Watts (W).

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ISSUE 4: Correcting SR-EM Hardware Mislabeling
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * What: Corrected the swapped registers for nodes SR-EM-KH04-01 and SR-EM-KH04-02:
      - freq_col_raw (0.02 - 0.25) -> renamed to current_A (Circuit Current).
      - voltage_V (~235 - 245 V)   -> preserved as Mains Voltage.
      - Extracted the raw "Power Factor" column from by_version (mean = 50.00 Hz), resampled it to
        15-min intervals, and added it as frequency_Hz.
  * Result: Sensor physical features accurately reflect true physical quantities.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ISSUE 5: Local Time Cyclical Temporal Feature Engineering
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * What: Converted UTC timestamps to Indian Standard Time (IST / Asia/Kolkata, UTC+5:30).
  * Encoded 6 continuous cyclical sine/cosine signals:
      - Hour of Day (hod_sin, hod_cos) : Period = 24.0 hours (96 steps of 15 min).
      - Day of Week (dow_sin, dow_cos) : Period = 7.0 days.
      - Day of Year (doy_sin, doy_cos) : Period = 365.25 days.
  * Result: Enables the LSTM to learn daily solar arcs and weekly building occupancy cycles continuously
    across boundary transitions (e.g., 23:45 to 00:00).

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ISSUE 6: Normalization & Invertible Scaling
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * What: Applied MinMaxScaler(feature_range=(0, 1)) to all continuous features and targets.
  * Why: Guarantees LSTM gates operate within their optimal, non-saturated linear gradient zones.
  * How Inversion Works: Exported scaler_params.json containing exact min, max, and scale values
    for every column of every node. Predictions can be converted back to real-world Watts in 1 line:
      Watts = pred_normalized * (max_val - min_val) + min_val

8.1 NET-ZERO PHYSICAL ENERGY BALANCE FORMULATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In building energy management and edge microgrid control, Net-Zero is defined by balancing demand
with on-site clean solar generation:

   1. Net Grid Deficit (W) = Campus Demand (W) - Solar PV Generation (W)
   2. Solar Self-Sufficiency Ratio (%) = [ Solar Generation (W) / Campus Demand (W) ] * 100%

Operational Interpretations:
   * When Solar >= Demand  : Campus is in NET-ZERO SURPLUS. 100% green self-sufficiency is achieved.
                             Excess clean power can either charge battery energy storage systems (BESS)
                             or export to the main utility grid.
   * When Solar < Demand   : Campus is in NET DEFICIT. On-site solar is insufficient to cover loads,
                             requiring residual power import from the utility grid.

Note on Solar Zero-Clamping:
Neural networks can output small negative values during night hours due to slight weight oscillations.
Physics-informed zero-clamping: Solar_Yield = max(0.0, Solar_Yield) is strictly enforced in all edge
inference runners.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8.2 DUAL-HORIZON EDGE ARCHITECTURE: 1-HOUR DISPATCH VS. 24-HOUR DAY-AHEAD
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To serve real-world microgrid operations, two complementary multi-sensor models are deployed:

  A. Short-Term Real-Time Dispatch Model (Horizon H = 4 steps / +1 Hour):

     - Model: qidk_deployment/all_sensors_lstm_qidk.onnx (586 KB, input [1, 96, 58], output [1, 12])
     - Single-step Solar R^2: 0.9554, nMAE: 3.60%

  B. Day-Ahead Planning Forecaster (Horizon H = 96 steps / +24 Hours):
