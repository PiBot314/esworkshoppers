### In progress — simulated multi-year pre-training set (the "multiple years of data" idea)
- **Chosen simulator:** per-plant **clear-sky power envelope(month, slot) × ERA5 clear-sky index**. On 2025 ok-days:
  R²bin 0.71–0.76, daily R² 0.70–0.75 (NI01 bin low 0.49 from faults, daily 0.72 OK). Beats the plain
  NNLS direct/diffuse fit on daily R² and gives correct per-plant daily shapes by construction.
- **Clear-sky extended to 2015–2024:** `clearsky.py` range widened (2015-01-01 start); NASA monthly scaling is
  month-of-year so it carries to all years. **Next action: rerun `clearsky.py`** to regenerate
  `clearsky_15min.csv.gz` (2015–2026, 15-min) — was about to run when this snapshot was taken.
- **Then build `datasets/training_2025/simulate_plants.py`:**
  1. envelope table per plant from 2025 ok clear-sky bins (month × 15-min slot);
  2. ERA5 2015–2024 hourly shortwave → clear-sky index, forward/interp to 15-min;
  3. simulated 15-min per-plant power = envelope × CSI for 2015–2024 → pre-training parquet + coef/R² report + README.
  - Sub-hour shape from deterministic sun (15-min clear-sky), hourly cloud from ERA5. No campus-load/calendar in it
    (solar only). Document R² and that it's a *pre-training* set; real 2025 remains the only train/test target.

## Key learnings carried forward
- Guided LSTM > plain; irradiance + cloud cover are strong; same-shaped LSTMs for similar-data plants help.
- Per-row quant needs the bias flag (19→45 dB). x86 HTP emulator reproduces the board (50.1 vs 48.4 dB).
- NPU is 3–5 ms/window vs ORT 0.3–0.5 ms; its case is batching/fusion/CPU-offload, not raw latency.
- Check a vendor tool's own flags before blaming it; label small-sample conclusions provisional.
- oneM2M findings are private; the mentors' API is synthetic — both excluded from training by decision.
